Chaarvy's Reports section gives administrators ready-to-download summaries of key institutional data. Whether you need a snapshot of student intake for a given period or enrollment figures for a board meeting, the Reports page puts that information at your fingertips with a single click.

## Student Admissions Report

The Student Admissions Report consolidates enrollment data across your institution, giving you a structured view of admitted students and their program details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Reports">
    Go to **Admin → Reports** from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the Student Admissions Report">
    Find the **Student Admissions Report** card on the Reports page. This report aggregates student enrollment records across all programs and admission cycles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the report">
    Click the **Download** button on the report card. Chaarvy will generate the report and save it to your device as a spreadsheet file (Excel or CSV), which you can open in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or any compatible application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the report contents">
    The downloaded file contains student enrollment data including student names, program details, admission dates, and enrollment status. Use this data for internal analysis, accreditation submissions, or stakeholder reporting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Downloading the Student Admissions Report requires the `reports:download:studentAdmissionsReport` permission. If the download button is not visible or is disabled on your Reports page, contact your system administrator to have this permission granted to your account or role.
</Note>
