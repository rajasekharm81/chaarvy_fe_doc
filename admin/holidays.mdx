Your institution's holiday list serves as the official academic calendar that Chaarvy uses when generating timetables and recording attendance. Keeping it accurate ensures that non-working days are correctly excluded from schedules and that attendance reports reflect true figures.

## Adding a Holiday

Navigate to **Admin → Holidays** to manage your institution's holiday calendar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Holiday form">
    Click **Add Holiday** to open the holiday creation modal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the holiday name">
    Type the name of the holiday as you'd like it to appear in the calendar and in reports — for example, *Republic Day*, *Diwali*, or *Founder's Day*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the date">
    Choose the date of the holiday using the date picker. For multi-day holidays, add each day as a separate entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the holiday">
    Click **Save** to add the holiday to your calendar. It will appear in the holiday list immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Holidays

After holidays are added, you can review, update, or remove them at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="View the full holiday list">
    The Holidays page displays all holidays in chronological order, showing the name and date of each entry. Use this view to audit your calendar before a new academic term begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a holiday">
    Click the edit icon next to any holiday to open the update form. You can change the name, adjust the date, or correct any errors. Click **Save** to apply the changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a holiday">
    To delete a holiday that was added in error or is no longer applicable, click the delete option next to the entry. This action removes it from the calendar immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Holidays you add here are reflected across the entire platform. They are excluded from timetable generation so that no classes are scheduled on those days, and they are factored into attendance calculations so students are not marked absent on institutional holidays. Always review your holiday list at the start of each academic year.
</Note>
