The Schedule Planner gives you a clear, calendar-based view of your entire teaching timeline. From a single screen, you can plan upcoming sessions, update existing entries when your schedule changes, and generate ready-to-print question papers drawn directly from your topic question bank — all without leaving the planner.

## Viewing Your Schedule

Navigate to **Faculty → Schedule Planner** to open the calendar grid. Each cell in the grid represents a period slot on a specific date. Cells that already have a planned session display the subject and topic at a glance, so you can see your upcoming workload across the week or month at once.

Completed sessions are visually distinguished from upcoming ones, making it easy to track your progress through the syllabus.

## Planning a New Session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an empty period slot">
    On the calendar grid, click any empty cell for the date and period you want to schedule. The **Plan Schedule** drawer slides open on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the session details">
    In the drawer, select the following fields:
    - **Program** — the academic programme this session belongs to
    - **Segment** — the year or semester within that programme
    - **Subject** — the subject you will teach
    - **Topic** — the specific topic from the subject's syllabus
    - **Medium** — the language of instruction
    - **Section** — the student section attending this session

    If your timetable is already configured, some fields may be pre-filled automatically based on your assigned timetable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the session">
    Click **Save** to add the session to the calendar. The cell updates immediately to show the subject and topic you just planned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating a Scheduled Session

If you need to change any detail of an already-planned session — for example, swapping a topic or correcting the section — you can edit it directly from the calendar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the existing entry">
    On the calendar grid, click the cell of the session you want to edit. The **Update Schedule** drawer opens, pre-populated with the session's current details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update any of the fields — program, segment, subject, topic, medium, or section — as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to apply the update. The calendar cell reflects the new information right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Generating a Question Paper

The Schedule Planner integrates a Question Paper Generator that pulls questions directly from your topic question banks. Use it to compose and print exam papers without switching to a separate tool.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Question Paper Generator">
    From within the Schedule Planner, select the subject and class you want to create a paper for, then open the **Question Paper Generator** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select topics">
    You will see a list of topics associated with the chosen subject. Check the boxes next to every topic you want to include in the paper. Each topic shows the number of available questions in the bank, so you can plan coverage confidently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and print">
    Click **Preview** to see the composed question paper — complete with section headers and answer-space formatting. When you are satisfied, click **Print Question Paper** to send it to your printer or save it as a PDF.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your account has the **`schedule_planner:can_plan_others`** permission, you can plan and edit sessions on behalf of other faculty members. Administrators typically hold this permission and use it to manage department-wide timetables from a central location. If you believe you should have this access but do not, contact your institution's Chaarvy administrator.
</Note>
