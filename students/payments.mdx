The Payments module brings together everything your finance and administrative staff need to manage student fee collection. You can review a complete history of successful transactions, identify students with outstanding balances, accept payments at the counter, and generate official receipts — without switching between separate tools.

## Viewing Payment History

Navigate to **Student Management → Payments** to access the payments hub. Two lists give you a full picture of where every student account stands.

### Successful Payments

The **Student Payments List** shows all completed fee transactions. Each row displays the student's name, the amount paid, the payment date, and the transaction reference. Use the search bar and date filters to locate a specific payment quickly.

Select any row to open the **Payment Details** view, which shows a full breakdown of what was paid, when, and through which payment method.

### Pending Payments

The **Pending Payments** list shows students who have an outstanding balance — either because a fee is due or a previous payment was not completed. Entries here are the starting point for collecting a payment in person.

### Viewing Payment Details and Printing Receipts

From either list, open a transaction record to view the complete payment details. Once you are on the detail view, click **Print Receipt** to generate a formatted receipt you can hand to the student or file for your records.

## Collecting Payments

Use the following steps to accept a fee payment from a student who appears on the pending payments list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Pending Payments">
    Navigate to **Student Management → Payments → Pending Payments**. This list shows all students with amounts currently due.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search for the Student">
    Use the search bar to find the student by name or enrollment number. The list filters in real time as you type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the Fee Breakdown">
    Select the student's row to open their pending payment record. You will see a detailed breakdown of all outstanding amounts — course fees, book fees, add-on course fees, and any other applicable charges.

    Confirm the total with the student before proceeding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record the Payment">
    Click **Accept Payment** to open the payment entry panel. Enter the following details:

    - **Amount Received** — the amount the student is paying now. This can be the full balance or a partial instalment if your institution allows instalments.
    - **Payment Mode** — select the method used (cash, bank transfer, online payment, etc.).
    - **Transaction Reference** — enter a cheque number, UTR, or any reference number associated with the transaction.

    Click **Confirm** to record the payment. The transaction is saved immediately and the student's balance is updated.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Print the Receipt">
    After the payment is recorded, click **Print Receipt** to generate the official receipt. Hand a copy to the student and retain one for your records if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Accepting a payment requires specific permissions. Make sure your role includes:

  - **collect_payment:search_pending_payment** — to search for and view pending balances
  - **collect_payment:accept_payment** — to record a new payment transaction
  - **collect_payment:print_receipt** — to print a receipt after payment

  Contact your system administrator if any of these permissions are missing from your account.
</Note>

## Payment Receipts

A payment receipt is automatically available as soon as a transaction is recorded. Receipts can be printed from two places:

- **During payment collection** — click **Print Receipt** immediately after confirming a payment.
- **From payment history** — open any past transaction from the Student Payments List and click **Print Receipt** from the detail view.

Each receipt includes the student's name and enrollment number, the program they are enrolled in, a line-by-line fee breakdown, the amount paid, the payment mode and reference number, and the date and time of the transaction.

<Tip>
  If a student requests a duplicate receipt for a past payment, locate the original transaction in the **Student Payments List**, open the detail view, and use **Print Receipt** to reprint it at any time.
</Tip>
