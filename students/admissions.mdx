The Admissions module gives your institution a centralised view of every student enrollment. From here you can browse active and inactive admissions, search for specific students, update enrollment statuses, and print official admission letters — all without leaving the platform.

## Viewing the Admissions List

Navigate to **Student Management → Admissions** to open the admissions list. The page is divided into two tabs so you can focus on the records most relevant to you.

### Active and Inactive Tabs

- **Active** — displays all students who are currently enrolled and attending your institution.
- **Inactive** — displays students whose admissions have been deactivated or dropped. Use this tab to review historical records or reverse a status change if needed.

Switch between tabs at any time; both views share the same search and filter controls.

### Filters and Search

Use the search bar at the top of the list to look up a student by name or enrollment number. Additional filters let you narrow results by:

- **Program** — the degree or diploma the student is enrolled in
- **Segment** — the academic segment or year group
- **Medium** — the language medium of instruction
- **Section** — the class section assigned to the student

Apply one or more filters together to pinpoint the exact set of records you need.

### Viewing Admission Details

Select any row in the list to open the full admission record. The detail view shows the student's personal information, program details, address, add-on courses, and fee summary all in one place.

---

## Admission Statuses

Every admission record carries a status that reflects the student's current relationship with your institution.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | The student is currently enrolled and attending. |
| **Inactive** | The admission has been deactivated — the student is no longer attending but the record is retained. |
| **Dropped** | The student formally withdrew or was removed from the program. Dropped admissions appear in the inactive tab. |

Keeping statuses accurate ensures your reporting, fee calculations, and headcount figures stay reliable.

## Available Actions

Depending on your permissions, you can perform the following actions from the admissions list or the individual admission detail view.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Print Admission Letter" icon="print">
    Generate and print the official admission letter for a student to share with them or retain for institutional records.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Update Admission Status" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Change a student's status between active and inactive to reflect enrollment changes such as re-admission or temporary suspension.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Drop Admission" icon="user-minus">
    Mark an admission as dropped when a student withdraws or is removed from a program. This moves the record to the inactive tab.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

To perform an action, open the admission record and select the corresponding option from the actions menu at the top of the detail view.

---

<Note>
  The actions available to you depend on the permissions assigned to your role. Your administrator controls access to the following:

  - **admissions:list:view:active** — view the active admissions tab
  - **admissions:list:view:inactive** — view the inactive admissions tab
  - **admissions:update_admission_status** — change a student's enrollment status
  - **admissions:print_admission_letter** — generate and print an admission letter

  Contact your institution's system administrator if you need access to any of these actions.
</Note>
