The Referrals feature helps your institution understand which channels and sources are driving student enrollments. By tracking referral data in one place, you can evaluate the effectiveness of outreach efforts, recognise high-performing referral sources, and make more informed decisions about where to focus recruitment activities.

## Viewing the Referrals Summary

Navigate to **Admin → Referrals** to open the referrals dashboard. The summary table loads automatically and displays all referral records your institution has collected.

### What the Dashboard Shows

Each entry in the referrals list represents a distinct referral source or referrer. The dashboard includes:

- **Referral Source** — the name of the person, channel, or campaign that referred the student.
- **Referral Count** — the total number of students attributed to that source.
- **Enrolled Students** — of those referred, how many completed enrollment.
- **Conversion Rate** — the percentage of referred students who went on to enroll, giving you a quick measure of source quality.

Use the search bar to look up a specific referrer by name, or sort the table by count or conversion rate to identify your most productive sources at a glance.

### Working with Referral Data

You can select any referral source row to see a list of the individual students attributed to it. This lets you cross-reference referral records with admission data and verify attribution accuracy.

<Info>
  Referral data is recorded at the point of admission. Make sure staff filling in the admission form capture the referral source accurately so that the dashboard reflects a true picture of your enrollment channels.
</Info>

---

<Note>
  Access to the Referrals dashboard requires the **referrels:view:list** permission. If you do not see the Referrals option under the Admin menu, ask your system administrator to grant you this permission.
</Note>
