The Expenses module gives your accounts team a single place to record every outgoing payment made by the institution. Whether you're logging a utility bill, a vendor payment, or a staff reimbursement, Chaarvy keeps an organised, searchable history of all expenses alongside their supporting documents.

## Accounts Dashboard

Before diving into individual expense records, the **Accounts Dashboard** gives you a financial snapshot of the institution at a glance. Navigate to **Accounts > Dashboard** to see:

- An **overall expenses chart** that visualises spending trends over time.
- A **financial status overview** summarising income, expenditure, and balance figures.

Use the dashboard as your starting point each time you open the Accounts module — it surfaces the information you need to make quick decisions before drilling into specific records.

## Viewing Expenses

To see all recorded expenses, navigate to **Accounts > Expenses**. The expenses list displays every entry your team has logged, sorted by the most recent date by default.

From the list view you can:

- **Search** by keyword to locate a specific expense quickly.
- **Filter** by expense category, date range, payment mode, or beneficiary type to narrow down results.
- **Click any row** to open the full expense detail and view attached receipts or invoices.

## Adding an Expense

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Expense form">
    From the **Accounts > Expenses** page, click the **Add Expense** button in the top-right corner. A form panel will open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an expense category">
    Choose the appropriate **Expense Category** from the dropdown. Categories help you group and report on similar types of spending. If the category you need does not exist yet, see [Expense Categories and Payment Modes](#expense-categories-and-payment-modes) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount, date, and payment mode">
    Fill in the **Amount** and select the **Date** the expense was incurred. Then choose the **Payment Mode** (for example, cash, bank transfer, or cheque) that reflects how the payment was made.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a beneficiary type">
    Pick the **Beneficiary Type** that best describes who received the payment — such as a vendor, staff member, or utility provider. This field is used for reporting and audit purposes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach receipts or invoices">
    Click **Upload Files** to attach supporting documents such as receipts, invoices, or purchase orders. You can attach multiple files in common formats (PDF, JPG, PNG). All attachments are stored securely and can be retrieved at any time from the expense record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the expense">
    Review the details and click **Save**. The new expense immediately appears in the expenses list and contributes to the figures shown on the Accounts Dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing an Expense

To correct or update an existing record, locate the expense in the list and click the **Edit** (pencil) icon. The same form used for adding an expense reopens with the existing values pre-filled. Make your changes and click **Save** to apply them.

<Warning>
  Editing a saved expense changes the figures reflected on the Accounts Dashboard. Always double-check the amount and date before saving an update.
</Warning>

## Expense Categories and Payment Modes

Keeping your categories and payment modes up to date ensures that every new expense can be classified accurately.

### Creating an Expense Category

1. On the **Accounts > Expenses** page, open the **Settings** or **Manage Categories** option (accessible from the page actions menu).
2. Click **Add Category**, enter a descriptive name (for example, *Office Supplies*, *Maintenance*, or *Utilities*), and save.
3. The new category is immediately available in the expense form dropdown.

### Adding a Payment Mode

1. In the same settings area, switch to the **Payment Modes** tab.
2. Click **Add Payment Mode**, enter the mode name (for example, *NEFT*, *Cash*, or *Credit Card*), and save.
3. The new mode appears in the **Payment Mode** dropdown when logging an expense.

<Info>
  You can also add a new **Beneficiary Type** from the settings area. Beneficiary types work the same way — enter a name that describes the recipient category, save, and it becomes available institution-wide.
</Info>

## Permissions

<Note>
  To add or edit expenses, your account must have the **expenses:add** and **expenses:edit** permissions assigned. To view the expenses list without making changes, the **expenses:view:list** permission is sufficient. Contact your system administrator if you cannot see the Add Expense button or the edit controls.
</Note>
