The Vendors module lets your accounts team build and maintain a complete directory of every supplier or service provider your institution works with. Keeping vendor records accurate and up to date makes it easy to reference the right contact when logging purchases, approving payments, or auditing expenditure.

## Viewing Vendors

Navigate to **Accounts > Vendors** to open the vendor list. Every vendor that has been registered in Chaarvy appears here, showing key details such as the vendor name, type, and contact information at a glance.

From the list view you can:

- **Search** by vendor name to locate a specific supplier quickly.
- **Click any vendor row** to open the full vendor profile, including all associated services and contact details.
- **Edit** an existing vendor record directly from its profile page.

## Adding a Vendor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Vendor form">
    From the **Accounts > Vendors** page, click the **Add Vendor** button in the top-right corner. The vendor form will open in a side panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the vendor name and contact details">
    Fill in the **Vendor Name** and provide the relevant **Contact** information — such as a phone number, email address, or representative name. Accurate contact details make it easy to reach the supplier when you need to follow up on orders or invoices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a vendor type">
    Select the appropriate **Vendor Type** from the dropdown. Vendor types act as broad categories that describe what kind of supplier the vendor is (for example, *Stationery Supplier*, *Canteen*, or *Maintenance*). If the type you need does not exist yet, see [Vendor Types and Services](#vendor-types-and-services) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the services provided by the vendor">
    Under the **Vendor Services** section, add one or more services that this vendor provides (for example, *Printing*, *Plumbing Repair*, or *Food Supply*). Listing services helps your team quickly identify which vendor to contact for a specific need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the vendor">
    Review all the details and click **Save**. The vendor is now registered in Chaarvy and appears in the vendor list for use across the Accounts module.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing a Vendor

To update an existing vendor's details, find the vendor in the list and click on their name to open the profile. Click the **Edit** button, make your changes, and click **Save**. Updates take effect immediately and are reflected wherever the vendor is referenced.

## Vendor Types and Services

Vendor types and services are institution-level reference data that you can configure to match your procurement structure.

### Vendor Types

Vendor types act as top-level categories for classifying suppliers. Examples include:

- *Stationery Supplier*
- *Canteen / Food Vendor*
- *Maintenance & Repair*
- *IT Hardware*
- *Transport*

To add a new vendor type, open the **Vendor Types** settings from the page actions menu, click **Add Vendor Type**, enter the name, and save. The type is immediately available when creating or editing a vendor.

### Vendor Services

Each vendor can have multiple services associated with their profile. Services describe the specific offerings a vendor provides, giving you finer granularity than the vendor type alone. For example, a vendor of type *Maintenance & Repair* might list services such as *Electrical Work*, *Plumbing*, and *Carpentry*.

To add a new service option, open the **Vendor Services** settings, click **Add Service**, enter the service name, and save.

<Tip>
  Link your vendor records to expenses whenever you log a payment. Selecting the vendor while recording an expense creates a clear audit trail between what was purchased, who supplied it, and how it was paid — making financial reviews and procurement reports significantly easier to produce.
</Tip>
