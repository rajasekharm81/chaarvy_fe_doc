The Timetable module in Chaarvy gives you a structured way to plan the weekly teaching schedule for every class in your institution. By combining period templates with subject-to-slot assignments, you can produce a complete, conflict-free timetable in minutes — and view any faculty member's full weekly schedule at a glance.

## Understanding Period Templates

Before you can assign subjects to time slots, Chaarvy needs to know what those time slots look like. A **period template** defines the daily schedule of periods for a class — the start time, end time, and label for each slot (for example, *Period 1: 9:00 AM – 9:50 AM*).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Timetable settings">
    From the Admin sidebar, go to **Academics → Timetable**, then open the **Period Templates** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new template">
    Click **Add Template**, give it a descriptive name (for example, *Standard Day — 6 Periods*), and add each period with its start time, end time, and period label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include breaks if needed">
    Add non-teaching intervals such as a lunch break or short recess as separate entries. Marking them clearly prevents subjects from being accidentally scheduled during those times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the template">
    Click **Save**. The template is now available to apply when creating timetables for any class.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You can create multiple period templates to accommodate classes that follow a different daily schedule — for example, a half-day schedule for certain programs or a different slot arrangement for lab-heavy courses.
</Info>

## Creating a Timetable

With a period template in place and subjects assigned to the relevant program segment, you are ready to build the timetable.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Timetable module">
    Navigate to **Academics → Timetable** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the class">
    Use the filters at the top of the page to choose the:
    - **Program** (for example, *B.Com*)
    - **Segment** (for example, *Semester 2*)
    - **Medium** (for example, *English*)
    - **Section** (for example, *Section A*)

    Chaarvy loads the timetable grid for that specific class combination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a period template">
    If no template is applied yet, select the appropriate **Period Template** from the dropdown. The timetable grid updates to show the correct number of periods per day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign subjects to slots">
    Click on an empty slot in the grid. A picker appears showing the subjects assigned to this program segment. Select the subject and the faculty member who will teach that slot, then confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the remaining slots">
    Repeat for every period across each day of the week. You can leave slots empty for free periods or breaks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the timetable">
    Click **Save Timetable** once all slots are assigned. The timetable becomes active and is visible to faculty and students.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checking Faculty Availability

Chaarvy automatically checks for scheduling conflicts as you assign subjects to slots. If a faculty member is already assigned to another class during the same period, the system flags the conflict before you can confirm the assignment.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Conflict detected" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    When you select a faculty member for a slot and they are already scheduled elsewhere at that time, Chaarvy displays a conflict warning. Choose a different faculty member or adjust the conflicting slot to resolve it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No conflict" icon="circle-check">
    When the selected faculty member is free during the chosen slot, the assignment proceeds normally and the slot is filled.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Reviewing availability before finalising the timetable prevents double-booking and ensures every class has a confirmed teacher for each period.

## Viewing the Faculty Timetable

Once timetables are saved, you can view an individual faculty member's consolidated weekly schedule across all the classes they teach.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Faculty Timetable view">
    In the Timetable module, switch to the **Faculty Timetable** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a faculty member">
    Choose the faculty member from the dropdown. Chaarvy displays their full weekly schedule, showing every class they are assigned to, the subject, and the time slot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and share">
    Use the timetable view to verify workload balance, identify free periods, or share the schedule with the faculty member directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Always click **Save Timetable** before navigating away from the timetable editor. Chaarvy does not auto-save slot assignments — any unsaved changes will be lost if you leave the page or refresh your browser.
</Warning>
