Subjects sit at the heart of your academic calendar in Chaarvy. Before you can build a timetable, record attendance, or organise teaching topics, each subject must exist in the system and be connected to the correct program segment and teaching faculty. This page walks you through the full lifecycle of a subject.

## Creating a Subject

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subjects module">
    From the Admin sidebar, navigate to **Subjects**. The list shows all subjects currently configured in your institution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Subject">
    Select the **Add Subject** button to open the subject creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter subject details">
    Fill in the required fields:
    - **Subject Name** — the full name as it should appear in timetables and reports (for example, *Mathematical Analysis* or *Organisational Behaviour*).
    - **Subject Code** — a short identifier used in timetable slots and transcripts (for example, *MA201*).
    - Any additional fields your institution has configured, such as credit hours or subject type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the subject">
    Click **Save**. The subject is now available to be assigned to program segments and faculty.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning Subjects to Program Segments

A subject must be mapped to a specific program segment before it can appear in that segment's timetable or attendance register. This mapping tells Chaarvy exactly which group of students is expected to study the subject.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subject">
    From the Subjects list, click the subject you want to assign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Program Assignments">
    Select the **Program Assignments** tab (or the equivalent section) within the subject detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the program and segment">
    Choose the **Program** from the dropdown, then choose the **Segment** (for example, *Year 2* or *Semester 3*) within that program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the assignment">
    Click **Assign**. The subject is now linked to that program segment and will be available when building the segment's timetable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat as needed">
    If the subject is taught across multiple programs or segments, repeat the steps above for each combination.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning Subjects to Faculty

Once a subject is linked to a program segment, you assign it to the faculty member responsible for teaching it. This drives faculty workload tracking, timetable conflict detection, and the attendance register.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subject">
    From the Subjects list, click the subject you want to assign to a faculty member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Faculty Assignments">
    Select the **Faculty Assignments** tab within the subject detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the program, segment, and faculty member">
    Select the **Program** and **Segment** combination, then pick the **Faculty** member from the list of available staff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the assignment">
    Click **Assign**. The faculty member is now responsible for that subject in the chosen segment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Subject assignments are used by two other Chaarvy modules automatically. When you build a **Timetable**, only subjects that have been assigned to the selected program segment are available to place in time slots. When faculty open the **Attendance Register**, they see only the classes for subjects assigned to them — so keeping assignments accurate is essential for both scheduling and daily attendance tracking.
</Note>
