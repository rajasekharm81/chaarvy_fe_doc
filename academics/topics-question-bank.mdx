The Topics and Question Bank module in Chaarvy helps faculty keep their teaching content organised and their exam preparation efficient. You create topics to structure what you teach within each subject, and then build a bank of questions under each topic that can be drawn on when generating question papers through the Schedule Planner. This page explains how to set up topics and populate the question bank.

## Creating Topics

Topics break a subject down into teachable units — similar to chapters or modules. Creating them in Chaarvy before adding questions keeps your question bank well-organised and makes it easy to pull questions on a specific area during exam planning.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Topics module">
    Log in as a faculty member and navigate to **Faculty → Topics** in the sidebar. You will see a list of all topics you have already created, grouped by subject.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the subject">
    Use the subject filter or dropdown to choose the subject for which you want to create a topic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Topic">
    Select the **Add Topic** button to open the topic creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter topic details">
    Fill in the following fields:
    - **Topic Name** — a concise, descriptive title (for example, *Demand and Supply Analysis* or *Introduction to Recursion*).
    - **Description** — a brief summary of what the topic covers. This helps colleagues and administrators understand the scope at a glance.
    - Any additional fields configured by your institution, such as teaching hours or learning outcomes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the topic">
    Click **Save**. The topic now appears under the selected subject and is ready for questions to be added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Building the Question Bank

Each topic has its own question bank. Adding questions here creates a reusable library that saves time every time you need to prepare an assessment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a topic's question bank">
    From the Topics list, find the topic you want to populate and click **Question Bank** (or the question bank icon) next to it. The question bank view opens, showing any questions already added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new question">
    Click **Add Question** to open the question entry form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the question type">
    Select the **Question Type** from the available options. Common types include:
    - **MCQ (Multiple Choice)** — provide the question stem and four or more answer options, then mark the correct answer.
    - **Short Answer** — provide the question and, optionally, a model answer or key points.
    - Additional types your institution may have configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a mark group">
    Assign the question to a **Mark Group** — for example, *1 Mark*, *2 Marks*, or *5 Marks*. Mark groups let you quickly filter and select questions of the right weightage when building a question paper.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the question content">
    Type the question text and, for MCQ questions, add all answer options and mark the correct one. Add any supporting notes or explanations for the model answer if useful.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the question">
    Click **Save**. The question is added to the topic's question bank and is immediately available for exam paper generation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for all questions">
    Continue adding questions until the topic's bank is complete. A richer bank gives you more flexibility when customising question papers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Bulk Operations

When you need to manage a large number of questions at once, Chaarvy provides two bulk operations to save time:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bulk Delete" icon="trash">
    Select multiple questions using the checkboxes, then click **Delete Selected** to remove them all at once. Use this to quickly clear outdated or duplicate questions from the bank.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bulk Update Question Type" icon="pen-to-square">
    Select multiple questions and use **Update Type** to change their question type in a single action — useful when reorganising a bank after a curriculum revision.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  The question bank you build here feeds directly into the **Schedule Planner** module, where Chaarvy can automatically assemble a question paper by drawing from this bank based on topic, question type, and mark group. The more comprehensive your question bank, the more useful the auto-generation feature becomes — aim to add at least ten questions per topic before your first exam cycle.
</Tip>
